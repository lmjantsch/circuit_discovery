## Patterns across (model, task)

- **`v` (value projection)**: scaling down to 0 collapses CPR everywhere — the value projection is the most load-bearing path. Slight upward scaling (`v=1.5`) is a small win on some blocks; `v=3.0` flat-lines.

- **`k` (key projection)**: reducing toward 0 tends to *help* — strongest on qwen2.5/ioi (`k=0.05`, +0.32). Large `k` (≥1.5) hurts.

- **`gate`**: on gpt2 has zero effect (no gated MLP architecture). On qwen2.5 and gemma2, low gate (0.05–0.2) is consistently the best perturbation in this sweep — gemma2/ioi with `gate=0.1` improves +0.68 CPR.

- **`q`**: similar to `v` — both extremes hurt; modest reduction (`q=0.5`) is sometimes a tiny win.

- **`up`**: usually flat. gpt2/ioi sees a +0.10 lift at `up=0.5`. Very large up (≥1.5) consistently hurts.
